### Chapter 4 - Decorators & Wrappers

4. Brand & UI Heuristics: Do's and Don'ts

Strict design rules derived from human-computer interaction (HCI) research and learner psychology.

✓ DO
Limit Warm Amber to ONE Focal Point

Reserve `#D9962B` strictly for the top urgent deadline, active streak flame, or chapter completion reward. Keep everything else grounded in calming Forest Green.

✗ DON'T
Don't Use Amber for Body Text or Backgrounds

Never use amber as body text on light backgrounds (it fails WCAG AA contrast under 3:1). Never use large amber splash cards that overwhelm peripheral vision.

✓ DO
Keep Body Text at 16px with 1.6 Line Height

Ensure long study sessions don't cause eye strain. Reading length must remain between 60–75 characters per line.

✗ DON'T
Don't Shrink Text Below 14px for Real Content

Never use 11px or 12px text for essential study instructions or notes. Captions and timestamps are the only exception.

✓ DO
Use Neutral, Encouraging Tutor Copy

"Welcome back. Every day you study counts. Let's pick up where you left off." Treat learners like intelligent adults.

✗ DON'T
Never Use Guilt-Inducing or Alarmist Warnings

Avoid toxic gamification: "You're falling behind!" or "Don't break your streak!". Genuine sick days and gaps must feel safe.
